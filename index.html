Privacy Policy

Who we are

Do Good Party Box is run from Colchester, Essex. We decide how your personal data is used. You can contact us at hello@dogoodpartybox.co.uk.

What information we collect

If you send us an unavailable date enquiry, we collect your name, email address, postcode, number of guests and any notes you provide.

If you make a booking, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, postcode, number of guests, party date, whether you want collection or delivery and the time window you choose, your delivery address and any delivery notes if you choose delivery, your kit colour and washing choices, any discount code you use, and your agreement to our hire terms.

When you pay, your card details go straight to Stripe, our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

We also record which party dates are searched on the website. This does not include any details that identify you.

How we use your information

We use your booking details to take your payment, reserve your kit, send your confirmation and receipt, send reminders about your booking (before your party and before the return date), arrange collection or delivery, deal with returns, including any late fees or lost or damaged items, and ask for your feedback once after your hire (you can tell us you would rather not be asked). We use enquiry details to reply to you. We only send marketing emails (about future availability, reminders and offers) to people who have ticked the optional box to keep in touch, on the booking form or the enquiry form. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us and we will stop. We use your details to deal with your booking or enquiry whether or not you tick it.

We keep records of bookings and payments for our accounts and tax.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use a small number of services to run the business, and they handle your data on our behalf: Stripe (payments), Google (Google Sheets, where we keep booking records), Zoho (sending our emails), Fly.io and GitHub (running this website and booking system), postcodes.io (looking up your postcode to work out delivery distance) and Slack (internal alerts to us). This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK.

How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry information for 12 months from the date you send it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. We keep booking and payment records for up to 6 years, because we need them for our accounts and tax, and then delete them.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to us contacting you. To exercise these rights, please email hello@dogoodpartybox.co.uk. We may need to keep booking and payment records for the period above even if you ask us to delete them. If you are unhappy with how we use your data, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Hire terms

The short version

  • Please return your kit by the return date in your confirmation email.
  • You get one day's grace. After that, a late fee of £5 per day applies.
  • Lost or broken items are charged at £1 each. Normal wear and tear is fine.
  • You can cancel for a full refund up to 1 day before your collection or delivery date. After that, the full booking amount is kept.

1. Your booking

These terms apply when you hire a party kit from Do Good Party Box, based in Colchester, Essex. Your booking is confirmed once your payment has been received and we have sent you a confirmation email. By ticking the box on the booking form you agree to these terms.

2. What is in a kit

Each kit includes a place setting for every guest you booked for (a plate, bowl, cup, knife, fork and spoon), plus 3 jugs, 3 trays, serving bowls and a tablecloth. Please check the contents against the list in the box when you receive it and tell us straight away if anything is missing.

The kit stays our property at all times and is for your own party use.

3. Collection, delivery and return

You can collect the kit from us or choose delivery, as selected when you booked. Your confirmation email shows the date your kit is due back, which is 3 days after your party date. If you chose delivery, we will collect the kit from you on that date.

Please return everything that came in the box.

4. Washing

If you chose our washing service, you do not need to wash anything before it goes back. If you did not, please wash everything properly with soap and water (not just a rinse) and dry it before you return it.

5. Late returns

Kits are often booked again straight away, so a late return can affect another family's party. You have one day's grace after the return date. After that, a late fee of £5 per day applies until the kit is back with us. We will let you know by email before we ask you to pay anything.

6. Lost or damaged items

We expect a little normal wear and tear, and that is fine. If items are lost or broken:

  • Plates, bowls, cups and cutlery are charged at £1 per item lost or broken.
  • Jugs, trays, serving bowls and tablecloths are charged at what it costs us to replace them. We will tell you the amount first.

We will check the kit when it comes back and email you if anything is missing or broken. We will send you a payment request by email for any amount owed.

7. Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel your booking for a full refund up to 1 day before your collection or delivery date. After that, we keep the full booking amount. To cancel, email us at hello@dogoodpartybox.co.uk with your name and party date.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with and usually take a few working days to appear.

If we ever have to cancel your booking, for example because of illness or a problem with the kit, we will refund you in full.

8. Discount codes

Discount codes reduce the kit hire price only. They do not apply to the washing service or delivery.

9. Your rights

Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights.

10. Contact

Questions? Email hello@dogoodpartybox.co.uk.
