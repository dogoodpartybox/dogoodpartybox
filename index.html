Privacy Policy

What information we collect

When you submit an unavailable date enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, postcode, number of guests, and any notes you provide. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry about party box availability.

How we use your information

We use your information to contact you about your specific enquiry and may contact you about Do Good Party Box offers or future availability. We do not share your information with third parties and do not use it for any purpose other than responding to your enquiry and marketing our services to you.

How long we keep your data

We retain your enquiry information for 12 months from the date of submission, unless you request deletion sooner.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to us contacting you. To exercise these rights, please email hello@dogoodpartybox.co.uk.
