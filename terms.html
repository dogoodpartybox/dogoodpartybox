Hire terms

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

  • Please return your kit by the return date in your confirmation email.
  • You get one day's grace. After that, a late fee of £5 per day applies.
  • Lost or broken items are charged at £1 each. Normal wear and tear is fine.
  • You can cancel for a full refund up to 1 day before your collection or delivery date. After that, the full booking amount is kept.

1. Your booking

These terms apply when you hire a party kit from Do Good Party Box, based in Colchester, Essex. Your booking is confirmed once your payment has been received and we have sent you a confirmation email. By ticking the box on the booking form you agree to these terms.

2. What is in a kit

Each kit includes a place setting for every guest you booked for (a plate, bowl, cup, knife, fork and spoon), plus 3 jugs, 3 trays, serving bowls and a tablecloth. Please check the contents against the list in the box when you receive it and tell us straight away if anything is missing.

The kit stays our property at all times and is for your own party use.

3. Collection, delivery and return

You can collect the kit from us or choose delivery, as selected when you booked. Your confirmation email shows the date your kit is due back, which is 3 days after your party date. If you chose delivery, we will collect the kit from you on that date.

Please return everything that came in the box.

4. Washing

If you chose our washing service, you do not need to wash anything before it goes back. If you did not, please rinse everything clean before you return it.

5. Late returns

Kits are often booked again straight away, so a late return can affect another family's party. You have one day's grace after the return date. After that, a late fee of £5 per day applies until the kit is back with us. We will let you know by email before we ask you to pay anything.

6. Lost or damaged items

We expect a little normal wear and tear, and that is fine. If items are lost or broken:

  • Plates, bowls, cups and cutlery are charged at £1 per item lost or broken.
  • Jugs, trays, serving bowls and tablecloths are charged at what it costs us to replace them. We will tell you the amount first.

We will check the kit when it comes back and email you if anything is missing or broken. We will send you a payment request by email for any amount owed.

7. Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel your booking for a full refund up to 1 day before your collection or delivery date. After that, we keep the full booking amount. To cancel, email us at hello@dogoodpartybox.co.uk with your name and party date.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with and usually take a few working days to appear.

If we ever have to cancel your booking, for example because of illness or a problem with the kit, we will refund you in full.

8. Discount codes

Discount codes reduce the kit hire price only. They do not apply to the washing service or delivery.

9. Your rights

Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights.

10. Contact

Questions? Email hello@dogoodpartybox.co.uk.
